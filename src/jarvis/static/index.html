What reaches the phone

Recording never changes: a mode only decides which messages are sent. From the phone, /home, /away and /silent switch it, and /mode shows it.

Case

Resident: marked on the person's or dog's page. A sure match is the resident; a "probably …" of a resident has a switch of its own, off in Home. When a mode leaves any person out, a person's message waits for the face scan and says who it was.

Telegram

Nothing is dropped. Messages less than this apart belong to one incident: its first rings, and so does another camera or more people at once; the rest arrive silently. At most one rings every 30 seconds; what comes inside is named by one text at the end.

Detection sensitivity

How sure YOLO must be before motion counts as a person or an animal. Slide left and it finds more, and more false ones; slide right and it is stricter and misses more. A change reaches the cameras within seconds, without a restart.

How the sliders work

Each slider moves YOLO's minimum confidence: the score, from 0 to 1, a box must reach before it counts. The middle is the default. The left end is and the right end , and each half moves in proportion between the default and its end, so the middle stays the default whatever its value.

    What this does not fix: a person bending over is called a dog as confidently as a real dog is, so moving the dog slider right cuts real dogs first. Those boxes are turned back into a person by where the person just stood, not by confidence.
